How it works

Four years. One master's. Five residencies.

Year 1

Foundations and Residency 1

Studio-based fundamentals in programming, systems and maths, then your first paid residency in the summer.

Year 2

Residencies 2 and 3

Human-centric computing and security alongside two more paid residencies inside partner engineering teams.

Year 3

Specialism and Residency 4

Scaling, reliability and a larger immersive software-project residency. Exit here with a BSc (Hons), or continue.

Year 4

Integrated MSc

AI and machine learning, advanced modules and a research-project residency, completing the integrated MSc.
