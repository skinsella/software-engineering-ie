How it works

Four years. Two degrees. Five residencies.

Year 1

Foundations

Studio-based fundamentals — programming, systems and maths — building real projects in small teams.

Year 2

First residencies

Paid residencies begin. Students join partner engineering teams for three to six months at a time.

Year 3

Depth & specialism

Deeper residencies paired with advanced coursework in a chosen area of software engineering.

Year 4

Master's project

A capstone master's project, often with an industry partner, completing the integrated MSc.
